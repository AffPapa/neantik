## Stop conditions and no-repeat rules

- Do not repeat v21/v22 headed scenarios, their Apple submissions, or full Swift/Python suites without changed relevant bytes, harness, environment, or a concrete failure.
- The v22 preflight reports incomplete transitive source/build inputs. Its missing independent recipient identity is no longer a release dependency; close the technical replacement proof in our own clean extraction instead.
- Production WebKit proxy stays disabled until its exact production runtime is qualified. A 1.0 label or public copy cannot substitute for the gates above.
- `WebKitProxySession.swift` is a separate DEBUG-only transport experiment; it is not the owned-WebKit browser route. The product route is `BrowserProcessManager.launchIsolatedWebKitProfile` → `WebKitLoopbackBroker` → signed owned host. Do not spend time trying to promote the experiment class or infer product readiness from its compile condition.
- Do not make another alpha release just to avoid this work. The existing alpha remains public until the one production candidate is ready; no outside person, new certificate or repeated v21/v22 test is required.
