NeAntik domain protection baseline

Source: CERT Polska Dangerous Websites Warning List
https://cert.pl/en/warning-list/
https://hole.cert.pl/domains/v2/domains.txt

CERT permits use of its list, including commercial use. CERT does not endorse
NeAntik. The source primarily covers known campaigns targeting Polish users;
it is not a complete malware detector. HTTPS transport is not a publisher
signature. The public snapshot provenance is in baseline-provenance.json.
The extension refreshes the list every five minutes while the browser runs.
No visited URL, page content, cookie or account is sent to the list provider.

The small tracker list is independently maintained here and is not EasyPrivacy.
Other extension code: NeAntik project license.
