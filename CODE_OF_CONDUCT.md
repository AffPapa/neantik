# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
publication of personal data, and attempts to obtain credentials are not
accepted.

Keep technical discussions focused on legitimate privacy, development, QA,
accessibility, reliability, and security. Requests whose purpose is bypassing
CAPTCHAs, bans, anti-fraud controls, or third-party platform rules are out of
scope.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close contributions that violate these rules.
